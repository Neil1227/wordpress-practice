Treatment Overview & Clinical Significance

SLT targets the eye’s natural drainage system to improve fluid outflow and safely lower pressure without damaging surrounding ocular tissue.

Targeted Laser Approach

The 532 nm Q-switched Nd:YAG laser delivers microscopic light pulses specifically absorbed by melanin-rich pigmented cells in the trabecular meshwork, stimulating natural biological drainage without thermal damage.

Non-Invasive & Pain-Free Nature

Unlike conventional surgical procedures, SLT requires zero surgical incisions. Topical numbing drops ensure complete comfort throughout the 5-minute session.

Repeatability & Long-Term Safety

Because cellular architecture is preserved rather than scarred, the procedure can be safely repeated in subsequent years if intraocular pressure rises again.

Patient Eligibility & Criteria

Patient Eligibility & Contraindications

Understanding whether SLT is the ideal first-line or complementary therapy ensures optimal outcomes and lasting vision preservation.

Candidates for SLT
  • Primary open-angle glaucoma or ocular hypertension
  • Patients experiencing side effects or intolerance to eye drops
  • Difficulty maintaining daily drop routines due to memory or dexterity
  • Need for added pressure reduction following prior cataract surgery
Contraindications

SLT is generally not recommended for active uveitic/inflammatory glaucoma, congenital conditions, or closed-angle glaucoma where the drainage angle cannot be visualized.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are clear, authoritative answers to our patients’ most common inquiries regarding laser treatment.

Can SLT be used as a first-line treatment?

Yes. Major clinical trials (such as the LiGHT Study) confirmed that SLT provides equal or superior pressure control compared to initial drop therapy, saving patients years of medication costs.

Is SLT laser treatment painful?

No. Topical anesthetic eye drops completely numb the surface of the eye. Patients typically feel only slight pressure from the examination lens.

Is the procedure covered by health insurance?

Yes. SLT is a medically necessary clinical procedure approved and covered by Medicare, Medicaid, and virtually all major commercial insurance plans.
